## Current Projects

### CaseTrace

An experimental cybercrime reconstruction challenge in which participants analyse real-world case material, reconstruct the incident, evaluate digital evidence, distinguish facts from inference, and make only evidence-supported attribution claims.

Currently under pilot development.

### Social Engineering Incident Atlas India

A developing structured dataset and research project examining publicly documented social-engineering-enabled cybercrime cases in India.

The project will study attack methods, impersonation strategies, communication channels, digital evidence, financial trails, attribution methods, and recurring investigative patterns.

### PAL V2

A browser privacy research framework studying fingerprint stability, privacy drift, and linkability across modern browser contexts.

[View PAL V2](https://github.com/stuhamz/PAL_V2)

### eCourts Scraper

A research-oriented toolkit for retrieving and organising Indian court information.

I am exploring its extension toward cybercrime case discovery, electronic-evidence research, and structured analysis of Indian cybercrime jurisprudence.

[View eCourts Scraper](https://github.com/stuhamz/ecourts-scraper)
